The library village

Dungine.v3, 4 October 2026. The Village of Barovia, built from the library's village kit, in the game.

The village's houses, tavern, shop, manor and church are now the library's, from the kit you approved. Its fences, walls, gates, well, market stalls, notice board and statue are the library's too. Each building stands where v2 had it, within a few centimetres, so the streets, the square and everything that happens there are as before. The ground, the roads, the trees, the lamps and the fog are still v2's for now; the village's own ground comes next. The second looks you asked to be darker and more worn, and the manor's hill, will appear when the layout is updated.

Walking through

The same walks, before and after, at the game's camera: up the high street from the south road to the square, from the square to the church gate, and from the square to the manor gate. Real speed.

Around the village

The game camera zoomed out as far as it goes.

Seeing the party

When a building stands between the camera and the party, it opens a see-through hole round them, as v2's buildings did.

Doors

The tavern and the shop are smaller than v2's, and some houses face a little differently, so many doors moved by up to three metres. Every door now follows the library building's own door. The party walks into the tavern, the shop, the manor and the church as before, and comes back out in front of the same door. Every house door can be reached, including five that v2's own houses had blocked.

Speed

The library village has far more detail than v2's. Near the party every building shows its full detail; further away each building turns into a simpler version, and far off into a single block. The game still runs at about the same speed in the village on this PC; the numbers are in the notes. There is room to make it cheaper: the buildings' shadows cost the most, and the shadows can use a simpler version.
