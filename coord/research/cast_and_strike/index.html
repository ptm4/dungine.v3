Cast and strike

Dungine.v3, 3 October 2026. Your notes on the cast and the strike, redone.

Each picture shows the library's fighter on the left and one of v2's own heroes on the right, each from the side and at the game camera's height. The game camera's view is turned so the arms aren't pointing straight at you. Everything plays at real speed. The same motions drive both kinds of figure, so v2's heroes get the new motions too.

Cast A, the throw

The casting hand tucks under the other armpit, then sweeps out to full reach at the target, like a throw. The other arm stays straight out at the target the whole time, and the elbows stay close in. Bolt spells use this one, the ones aimed at one target (Fire Bolt, for example).

Cast B, the push

Both hands come together at the chest, as if holding a ball, then push it straight out to full reach. Spells that land on an area or a spot use this one (Entangle or Moonbeam, for example).

A staff (or any weapon held in both hands) passes to the steadying hand for a cast and stands upright in it. Before, it swung through the body with the casting hand. It goes back to both hands afterwards.

The strike

A fighter's stance. The left arm is straight out at chest height as a guard, and the right hand is the striker, held low by the ribs with the blade pointing at the foe. The combo: a swing to the left, a swing to the right, then a stab straight forward as the third and final blow. The blade sweeps flat through the swings and points ahead for the stab.

The stance and the combo are for one-handed weapons, a sword and shield, and empty hands. Two-handed weapons and polearms (a greatsword, Arkus's glaive) keep their own stance and swings, with both hands on the weapon.
