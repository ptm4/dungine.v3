The game export in v3

Dungine.v3, 3 October 2026. The game now uses the library's new export of the figures.

The library now makes a version of each figure just for the game. Steel and gold now shine, nooks and creases are a little darker, and each figure comes in three levels of detail. The game now uses these files for every library figure.

The dark streaks

On curved surfaces, like the skirt of a dress, the small sides of the voxel steps that face away from the light came out dark, as thin dark stripes. The game's lighting now lets light wrap a little further round each face and adds a little more soft light from the sky. The stripes fade, and the blocks keep their shape.

Cloth

A dress or an apron that covers both legs now hangs in one piece from the hips. It no longer stretches into a sheet between the legs as they step, and the hem no longer flicks out with the shins.

Sitting and crouching: the middle of an apron or a dress hangs straight down between the knees, and the knees stay outside it, so nothing pokes through. The apron doesn't lie across the lap, though: it drops from the waist, under the thighs.

The fighter's cape in the new casts

The half-cape still lies over the right arm, so when that arm reaches out it is wrapped in red like a sleeve. In cast A, it also spreads across the chest as the hand tucks under the other arm.

Levels of detail

Each figure has three versions: the full one (1.5 cm blocks), one with blocks twice as big, and one with blocks four times as big. The game picks one by how big the figure is on screen. At the game camera's normal distance, the figures around the party use the full version. The middle one shows when the camera is zoomed far out, and the coarsest only when a figure is tiny on screen.
