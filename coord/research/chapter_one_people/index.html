Chapter One's people

Dungine.v3, 3 October 2026. The library's figures in the game, where v2 puts each person.

Everyone the party meets by name in Chapter One is now their library figure, in the same place and doing the same thing as in v2: standing, or seated at a table. They breathe and shift as v2's figures do, and keep the pose from their review (folded arms, a cup in hand) while they stand still. v2's village folk, who have no library figure yet, are still v2's.

Weapons and things in hand

When a library figure carries a weapon, it now carries the library's own model of that weapon. v2 still decides how it is held, drawn and swung, including both hands on a two-handed weapon. Ismark and Ireena already wear their swords in scabbards, so the sword in the hand appears only when they draw it, and they never have two.

The things the people carry in their reviews are in their hands: Arik's cup, Alenka's wine jug and Parriwimple's two sacks. They stay upright as the figure moves.
